Save your E&D notes to Google Drive

Set this up once, and your notes are the same on every computer you use. They are saved in your own Google Drive. Nobody else can see them, including whoever made this page.

Part 1: Set up Google

Do this one time only, on any computer. About 5 minutes. Use Chrome.

  1. Open script.google.com in a new tab, sign in with your Google account, and click New project (top left).
  2. Click this button to copy the script:
    Or open the script as text
  3. Go back to the Google tab. Click on the text in the middle, select all of it (Ctrl+A, or Cmd+A on Mac), and paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V). This replaces the old text. Then save (Ctrl+S / Cmd+S).
  4. At the top, the drop-down next to Debug should say setup. Click Run.
    • Google asks for permission. Click Review permissions and pick your account.
    • If it says “Google hasn’t verified this app”: click Advanced (small text, bottom left), then Go to Untitled project (unsafe). This is safe, because the script is your own.
    • Click Allow.
  5. At the bottom of the screen (Execution log) you will see YOUR SYNC KEY IS: …. Copy that key and keep it somewhere private. You need it in Part 2.
    • No key showing? Click Run again.
  6. Click the blue Deploy button (top right), then New deployment.
    • Click the gear icon next to “Select type” and choose Web app.
    • Execute as: Me
    • Who has access: Anyone (this only means the link works without signing in; without your key nobody can open your notes)
    • Click Deploy.
  7. Google shows a Web app link ending in /exec. Click Copy and keep it with your key.

Part 2: Turn on sync

Do this on each computer you use. Under a minute.

  1. Open the E&D page.
  2. Click Google Drive sync near the top.
  3. Paste the /exec link from Part 1, then click OK.
  4. Enter your sync key, then click OK.
  5. You should see “Google Drive sync is on”. Done.

Every day

  • Just use the E&D page. It saves to your Google Drive by itself.
  • Under the title it says “Synced with Google Drive at (time)” when everything is saved. Wait for this before closing the page.
  • Try not to type on two computers at the same time.
  • For privacy, the page signs you out by itself after 30 minutes without use, and when you close the browser. Your notes stay safe in Google Drive and come back when you open the page again. If some changes had not reached Google Drive yet (for example, no internet), they are kept on that computer and upload the next time you open the page there.

If something goes wrong

“Could not reach Google Drive, it will retry”
No internet. Your notes are still saved on this computer and upload by themselves when the internet is back.
“The key is wrong”
Click Fix Google Drive key and enter it again. Capital and small letters matter. Forgot it? In script.google.com, choose showKey in the drop-down and click Run.
“The script is not set up yet”
Do Part 1 step 4 again (choose setup, click Run).
“Could not reach the script”
The link is wrong, or “Who has access” was not set to Anyone. Check the link, or do Part 1 step 6 again.
The other computer doesn’t show the new notes yet
Click Sync now, or wait one minute.
Keep your link and key private. Together they open your patient notes. If you think someone else has them, in script.google.com choose newKey, click Run, and enter the new key on each computer. You are responsible for the patient data in your Google account, so follow your clinic’s rules.
