# E&D Notes

Examination & Diagnosis notes in the browser.

**Open the app:** the GitHub Pages link for this repository. The page is password protected: ask the person who shared it for the password.
**Save to your own Google Drive:** follow [setup.html](setup.html) (about 5 minutes, one time).

Your notes are never sent to this repository or its owner. They are kept in your browser and,
if you turn on sync, in `ed-notes-data.json` in your own Google Drive, through a script that runs
in your own Google account ([drive-sync-script.txt](drive-sync-script.txt)).
